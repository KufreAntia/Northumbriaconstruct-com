03 · Construction safety & training

Choose the right test, course or qualification.

These serve different purposes. Confirm the requirements for your role, chosen card and site before booking.

Test · CITB

Health, Safety & Environment Test — Operatives

An assessment of health, safety and environmental knowledge for general site workers.

Official Operatives test guidance ↗

Test · CITB

Health, Safety & Environment Test — Managers & Professionals

An assessment covering management, occupational health, safety, high-risk work and the environment.

Official Managers & Professionals test guidance ↗

Course · CITB Site Safety Plus

Site Management Safety Training Scheme — SMSTS

A five-day course addressing the management of construction site risks.

Official SMSTS course guidance ↗

Course · CITB eCourses

Health and Safety Awareness — HSA

An online introductory course on construction hazards and responsibilities. Its course assessment is separate from the HS&E test.

Official CITB Health and Safety Awareness eCourse ↗

Qualification · CSCS guidance

Level 1 Award in Health and Safety in a Construction Environment

A qualification accepted within the CSCS Labourer card pathway. It is distinct from CITB’s HS&E tests and HSA course; check CSCS guidance for accepted qualifications and alternatives.

Official CSCS qualification and Labourer card guidance ↗

Completing a course or qualification alone does not satisfy every CSCS card or site-access requirement. Follow the current card scheme requirements and the site’s induction and access arrangements.
