Bridging industry and education at Gateshead College

Northumbria Construct supported CIOB North East Tomorrow’s Leaders at Gateshead College, connecting approximately 40 students with industry professionals and university volunteers. Interactive careers discussions, the BRIC pilot and a building challenge brought career pathways, collaboration and practical learning together.
