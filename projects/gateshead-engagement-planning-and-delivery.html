Planning and delivering the Gateshead engagement day

Northumbria Construct contributed stakeholder coordination, volunteer mobilisation, planning and resources to the Gateshead professional engagement day. Explore how the team supported delivery, tested BRIC and NorCon Projects in practice, and used the experience to learn about accessible engagement and project governance.

Project · Newcastle Hub

CIOB North East Tomorrow’s Leaders Professional Engagement Day · Gateshead College · 7 October 2026

Northumbria Construct contributed to the planning, coordination and delivery of the professional engagement day involving CIOB North East Tomorrow’s Leaders and Gateshead College.

Coordinating participation

Our contribution covered stakeholder coordination, volunteer mobilisation, project planning, activity preparation and resource coordination. NorCon Projects was used to support the work, alongside the BRIC construction careers activity.

Contributions and learning

The engagement involved CIOB Tomorrow’s Leaders, Gateshead College, Northumbria Construct and individual volunteers. Northumbria Construct’s contribution forms part of that collective delivery.

Delivery and learning

Callum O’Connor led Northumbria Construct’s volunteer team, alongside Vemula Sai Praneeth, Finn Mulholland, Aditya Shetty, Mayuri Shinde and Collins Tamunotonye. Activities drew on the CIOB Think Construction Toolkit and piloted BRIC with approximately 40 students across eight teams.

The pilot identified opportunities to simplify BRIC instructions and refine gameplay. Volunteers put leadership, communication, teamwork and stakeholder engagement into practice; the experience reinforced the value of participatory learning and accessible professional networks.

Testing project governance in practice

Version 2 of NorCon Projects supported the contribution, building on the debate application with baselines and governance, risk and issue management, change control, benefits and value management, sustainability tracking and enhanced reporting.
