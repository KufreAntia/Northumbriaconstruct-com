Innovation

Tools, methods and solutions developed through research and practice, including BRIC and NorCon Projects.

Innovation · First pilot: 7 October 2026 · Physical pilot · further development

BRIC — Build Routes Into Construction

BRIC is an interactive educational card game helping students explore credible routes into construction careers. Teams combine qualifications, apprenticeships and practical experience, adapt to challenges and discuss progression. A Gateshead College pilot is informing clearer instructions, refined gameplay and future educational development.

Innovation · No public release date announced · In development and testing

NorCon Projects

NorCon Projects is a lightweight project management application for small teams delivering social-impact projects. Successive versions have been tested through the Oxford-Style Debate and Gateshead engagement day, bringing planning, coordination, reporting, accountability and sustainability tracking together through iterative development and practical learning.

Our purpose

From interest
to action.

We believe promising interests should have a clear route from ideation to action.

With the right support, networks and collaboration, ideas can develop into practical responses to real industry challenges.

The Innovation Lab creates space for interests, ideas and project experience to develop into useful technologies, frameworks and solutions that improve how projects are planned, delivered and managed.
