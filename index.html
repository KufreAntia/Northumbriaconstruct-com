About Northumbria Construct

Together, we can be the bridge.

Northumbria Construct is a community of students connecting with academia, industry and professional institutions through meaningful engagement, shared knowledge and opportunity.

NC

Our story

It began as a project.

Founded by Kufre Usen Antia, Northumbria Construct grew from Bridge the Gap—an award-nominated entry in the Association for Project Management Project Management Challenge.

The project examined why talented students can struggle to move from higher education into professional practice. Instead of leaving the findings in a report, the team turned its recommendations into a community of students.

Today, students come together through Northumbria Construct to connect with people, institutions and ideas, making professional knowledge, networks and opportunities easier to access.

Our purpose

Bridging skills and generations.

Technical ability matters, but successful careers are also shaped by access to experience, mentors, networks and a clear understanding of professional pathways.

Northumbria Construct creates structured opportunities for students to connect with academia, industry, experienced professionals and professional institutions.

Through engagement, mentoring, research, volunteering and collaboration, we help students understand not only what they can become—but how to get there.
