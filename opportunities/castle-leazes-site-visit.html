Castle Leazes — CIOB site visit

Join a guided visit to Bowmer & Kirkland’s student accommodation project in Newcastle. Organised through CIOB North East’s Constructing the North East programme, the opportunity offers a view of live construction. Consult the official CIOB listing for booking and attendance requirements.
