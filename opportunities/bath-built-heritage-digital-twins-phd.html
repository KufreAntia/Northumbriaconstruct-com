People-Led Digital Twins for Preventive Conservation of Built Heritage Assets

Explore a University of Bath doctoral research opportunity bringing together digital twins, structural health monitoring and explainable artificial intelligence for built heritage conservation. Home and overseas applicants may apply subject to programme requirements. Funding is competitive, and applications close on 29 November 2026.

Research / Education · Competitive PhD studentship

Offered by the University of Bath, UK
Department of Architecture and Civil Engineering

Application deadline
29 November 2026
Expected commencement
4 October 2027
Research supervisors
Dr Alejandro Jiménez Rios
Professor Paul Shepherd
Funding
Competitive funded PhD studentship
Eligibility
Home and overseas applicants may apply, subject to programme requirements and international studentship allocation limits.

Explore how digital technologies can support the conservation and management of historic buildings and infrastructure. This University of Bath PhD investigates people-led digital twins for preventive conservation of built heritage assets.

The research brings together structural health monitoring, sensor data, inspection records, interactive visualisation and explainable artificial intelligence to inform conservation decisions.

Human-in-the-loop approaches keep engineers, conservation specialists and other stakeholders involved in interpreting information, assessing asset conditions and deciding when to intervene. The project supports professional judgement in managing historic structures.

Who might be interested?

  • Built heritage conservation and restoration
  • Structural engineering and asset management
  • Digital twins and BIM
  • Structural health monitoring
  • Artificial intelligence and explainable decision-support systems
  • Sustainable management of historic infrastructure

Competitive funding

The project is competing for a studentship under the University of Bath's Faculty of Engineering and Design. Successful studentship recipients are expected to receive tuition-fee support, a maintenance stipend and a training-support allowance, subject to the published funding conditions. Funding is competitive and an award is not guaranteed.

Northumbria Construct shares this external opportunity for information. The studentship is independent of Northumbria Construct and the Heritage Walk. See the linked opportunity listing for complete eligibility, funding and application requirements.
