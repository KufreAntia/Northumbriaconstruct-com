Quayside Heritage Walk — Exploring Newcastle’s Built Heritage

Explore the architectural and industrial history of Newcastle and Gateshead through a guided Quayside walk led by Aditya T. Shetty. Discover historic buildings, infrastructure, heritage conservation and regeneration while connecting with students and built-environment enthusiasts. Participation is free and open to everyone.

Heritage, industry and regeneration

From historic merchant houses and industrial infrastructure to contemporary regeneration and adaptive reuse, the walk examines how the built environment reflects the city’s changing economic and social history.

Participants will explore landmarks including the Gateshead Millennium Bridge, BALTIC Centre for Contemporary Art, Tyne Bridge, The Close and Sandhill.

The event provides an informal opportunity to learn about heritage conservation, construction history and urban regeneration while connecting with fellow students and built-environment enthusiasts.

The walk is open to everyone. Optional social drinks may follow.

Meet your walk lead

Aditya T. Shetty

Aditya T. Shetty is a built-environment professional with a specialist interest in the sustainable restoration and conservation of historic buildings. His work explores conservation decision-making, historic fabric preservation and the balance between heritage protection and contemporary sustainability requirements.

In June 2026, Aditya participated as a panellist in the Chartered Institute of Building (CIOB) discussion, EPCs & Proposed Changes: What They Mean for Listed Buildings in England, contributing perspectives on sustainable retrofit, conservation and historic-building performance.
