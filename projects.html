Bridge the Gap

The founding Bridge the Gap project investigated barriers to student engagement with the construction industry through research, stakeholder engagement, interviews and educational outreach. Its learning about opportunity visibility, understanding and accessibility shaped a continuing programme and the establishment of Northumbria Construct.
