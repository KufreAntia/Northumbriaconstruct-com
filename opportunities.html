Built heritage opportunities

Built heritage presents opportunities to explore the history of our cities, understand the conservation of existing structures and contribute to research shaping their future.

From guided exploration of Newcastle's historic Quayside to doctoral research into people-led digital twins for heritage conservation, these opportunities highlight different ways to engage with the preservation and sustainable management of our built environment.

The connection between these opportunities is thematic. The University of Bath PhD is an independent external research opportunity signposted by Northumbria Construct.

CIOB visits: book through the official listings on each post.
